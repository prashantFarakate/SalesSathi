## Overview
The Tata Nexon.ev is a compact electric SUV and one of India's best-selling EVs. It is offered in two battery pack options: Medium Range (MR) and Long Range (LR).

## Battery and Range
- Medium Range (MR): 30 kWh battery pack, ARAI-certified range of approximately 325 km on a single charge.
- Long Range (LR): 45 kWh battery pack, ARAI-certified range of approximately 465 km on a single charge.
- Real-world range typically ranges from 250 km (MR) to 375 km (LR) depending on driving style, air-conditioning use, and terrain.

## Charging
- DC Fast Charging: 10% to 80% charge in approximately 40 minutes using a 50 kW DC fast charger.
- AC Home Charging (7.2 kW wall box): Full charge in approximately 6 hours for the Long Range variant.
- AC Home Charging (3.3 kW standard charger): Full charge in approximately 10 to 15 hours.
- The Nexon.ev supports both CCS2 fast charging and home AC charging.

## Performance
- Long Range peak power output: approximately 106 kW (about 142 bhp).
- Medium Range peak power output: approximately 95 kW (about 127 bhp).
- 0 to 100 km/h in approximately 8.9 seconds for the Long Range variant.
- Multiple drive modes: Eco, City, and Sport.

## Key Features
- Regenerative braking with multiple levels.
- 12.3-inch touchscreen infotainment on top variants with wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay.
- Air purifier, ventilated front seats, and electronic parking brake on higher trims.
- V2L (Vehicle to Load) capability to power external devices.
- 5-star Bharat NCAP safety rating.

## Variants and Indicative Pricing
- Nexon.ev Medium Range: starting around Rs 12.49 lakh (ex-showroom).
- Nexon.ev Long Range: ranging from Rs 14.99 lakh to Rs 17.19 lakh (ex-showroom).
- Prices are indicative and vary by city and offers.

## Warranty
- Battery warranty: 8 years or 1,60,000 km, whichever is earlier.
- Vehicle warranty: 3 years or 1,25,000 km standard, with extended warranty options.
