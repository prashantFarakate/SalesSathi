## Overview
The Tata Harrier is a premium mid-size SUV built on the OMEGARC platform derived from Land Rover's D8 architecture. It is positioned as a flagship SUV in Tata's internal combustion lineup, with a Harrier.ev electric variant also available.

## Engine and Performance (Diesel)
- Engine: 2.0-litre Kryotec turbocharged diesel.
- Peak power: approximately 125 kW (about 168 bhp).
- Peak torque: approximately 350 Nm.
- Transmission options: 6-speed manual and 6-speed torque converter automatic.
- Mileage: approximately 14.6 to 16.8 km/l (ARAI) depending on transmission.

## Harrier.ev (Electric)
- Battery options up to 75 kWh.
- Claimed range up to approximately 627 km (MIDC).
- All-wheel drive (QWD - Quad Wheel Drive) available on top variants via dual motors.
- DC fast charging 20% to 80% in approximately 25 minutes on a high-power charger.

## Key Features
- Panoramic sunroof on higher trims.
- 12.3-inch Harman infotainment touchscreen and 10.25-inch digital instrument cluster.
- ADAS Level 2 (Advanced Driver Assistance Systems) on top variants, including adaptive cruise control and lane keep assist.
- JBL premium sound system.
- Ventilated and powered front seats.

## Safety
- 5-star Bharat NCAP and Global NCAP safety rating.
- Up to 7 airbags, ESP, and multiple electronic safety aids.

## Variants and Indicative Pricing
- Harrier diesel: ranging from approximately Rs 15.49 lakh to Rs 26.44 lakh (ex-showroom).
- Harrier.ev: starting around Rs 21.49 lakh (ex-showroom).
- Prices are indicative and vary by city and offers.

## Warranty
- Vehicle warranty: 3 years or 1,00,000 km standard, with extended options.
- Harrier.ev battery warranty: 8 years or 1,60,000 km.
