## Overview
The Tata Punch is a sub-compact micro-SUV, one of the highest-selling cars in India. It is available with petrol, CNG, and fully electric (Punch.ev) powertrains.

## Punch (Petrol)
- Engine: 1.2-litre Revotron petrol.
- Peak power: approximately 65 kW (about 88 bhp).
- Transmission: 5-speed manual and AMT (automated manual transmission).
- Mileage: approximately 18.8 to 20.09 km/l (ARAI).

## Punch (CNG)
- Twin-cylinder CNG technology for a larger boot.
- Mileage: approximately 26.99 km/kg.

## Punch.ev (Electric)
- Battery options: Standard Range and Long Range.
- Standard Range: 25 kWh battery, range approximately 315 km (MIDC).
- Long Range: 35 kWh battery, range approximately 421 km (MIDC).
- DC fast charging 10% to 80% in approximately 56 minutes.
- AC 7.2 kW home charging: approximately 3.6 hours for a 10% to 100% charge on Long Range.

## Key Features
- 10.25-inch touchscreen infotainment on higher trims.
- Sunroof available on select variants.
- 5-star Global NCAP safety rating (petrol) and 5-star Bharat NCAP (Punch.ev).
- Multiple drive modes and regenerative braking on the EV.

## Variants and Indicative Pricing
- Punch petrol: ranging from approximately Rs 6.13 lakh to Rs 10.20 lakh (ex-showroom).
- Punch.ev: ranging from approximately Rs 10.99 lakh to Rs 15.49 lakh (ex-showroom).
- Prices are indicative and vary by city and offers.

## Warranty
- Vehicle warranty: 3 years or 1,00,000 km standard.
- Punch.ev battery warranty: 8 years or 1,60,000 km.
