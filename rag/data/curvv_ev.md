# Curvv EV — Product Brief

> Illustrative figures for demo purposes. Not verified production specs — replace with

- Segment: Coupe SUV
- Range: ~500 km claimed (MIDC test cycle), variant-dependent
- Battery: 45 kWh or 55 kWh (two variants)
- Fast charging: 0-80% in ~40 minutes (DC fast charger, 55 kWh variant)
- Price band: ₹17.5L – ₹22.0L (ex-showroom)
- Positioning: Style-led coupe SUV for customers who want the longest range
  in the lineup and a more premium design language than Nexon EV.
