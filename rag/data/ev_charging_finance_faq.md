# Tata Motors EV Charging, Finance, and Ownership FAQ

> Illustrative guidance for demo purposes. Confirm current figures with the
> dealership and finance team before quoting a customer.

## EV Charging - General
- Tata EVs use the CCS2 fast-charging standard, compatible with most public DC fast chargers in India.
- Home charging is done via an AC wall box (typically 3.3 kW or 7.2 kW) installed at the customer's residence.
- Tata Power and other partners offer a growing public charging network across major cities and highways.
- The Tata Motors ZConnect and companion apps allow remote monitoring of charge level and locating nearby chargers.

## Charging Time Guidance
- DC fast charging (50 kW+): most Tata EVs reach 10% to 80% in roughly 40 to 60 minutes.
- 7.2 kW home wall box: a typical full charge takes 4 to 6 hours depending on battery size.
- 3.3 kW standard home charging: a full charge can take 10 to 15 hours, best used overnight.

## Battery Health and Warranty
- All Tata EV batteries carry an 8 year or 1,60,000 km warranty (whichever is earlier).
- Battery management systems (BMS) protect against overheating and overcharging.
- Regenerative braking recovers energy during deceleration to extend range.

## Finance and EMI
- On-road price includes ex-showroom price plus road tax, registration, and insurance.
- A typical car loan covers up to 80% to 90% of the on-road price.
- EMI depends on loan amount, interest rate, and tenure. Example: for a loan of Rs 10 lakh at 9% annual interest over 5 years (60 months), the EMI is approximately Rs 20,760 per month.
- Many states offer EV subsidies and road-tax exemptions that reduce the effective on-road price.

## Test Drive and Booking
- Customers can book a test drive online through the Tata Motors website or at any authorized dealership.
- Booking amount for a new vehicle is typically a small token amount, refundable or adjustable against the final price.
- Delivery timelines vary by model, variant, and city inventory.

## Service and Ownership
- Tata Motors offers connected car features on many models via the ZConnect platform.
- Standard vehicle warranty is 3 years, with extended warranty and annual maintenance contracts available.
- Roadside assistance is offered as part of ownership packages on most models.
